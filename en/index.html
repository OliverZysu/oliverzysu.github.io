About Me

I am Ziyang Su, a direct-entry Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at the John Hopcroft Center for Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Associate Professor Guanjie Zheng. I am a member of City Intellingence LAB (CILAB).

My main research areas are large-scale graph neural network optimization, traffic flow generation, and product recommendation. My work spans city-scale road network data, POI-based traffic flow generation, and lifetime value prediction for multi-business transactions.

I earned B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology at Yingcai Honors College, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, 2022.

Selected Publications

The Web Conference 2027

CoGSeq-LTV: Scalable Missing-Aware Graph-Sequence Modeling for Multi-Business Transaction Analytics [paper][code]

Ziyang Su, Yao Zhou, Kelin Liu, Guanjie Zheng*, Linghe Kong

WWW2027 (CCF-A)·First author

CoGSeq-LTV combines a batch-induced user-item graph, business-specific sequence encoders, and cross-view contrastive learning for missing-aware, multi-business, multi-horizon lifetime value prediction. A zero-inflated mixture log-normal head models sparse, heavy-tailed spending, with evaluation on real transaction logs and a later out-of-time window.

The Web Conference 2027

DualClusterNet: Personalized Pattern-Space Composition for Next-POI Recommendation [paper][code]

Ziyang Su, Guanjie Zheng*, Jilin Hu, Linghe Kong

WWW2027 (CCF-A)·First author

DualClusterNet softly retrieves shared pattern and spatial prototypes at each trajectory prefix and composes them through a factorized multiplicative interaction. User-specific affine modulation, a separate personalized branch, and gated additive and projective fusion retain individual preferences while reusing population-level patterns for full-candidate next-POI recommendation.

The Fifteenth International Conference on Learning Representations

Multi-task Prediction of Solid-phase Extraction Protocol Components from Molecular Descriptors [paper][code]

Ziyang Su, Kun Zhang, Guanjie Zheng*, Bin Lu, Meng Jin, Xinbing Wang

ICLR2027·First author

SPEGen jointly predicts solid-phase extraction cartridges, workflow steps, solvent sets, and ratios from molecular descriptors, using set-valued supervision to retain reported alternatives and masking missing ratio annotations. Rank-Gauss normalization, validation-tuned decoding, and bootstrap aggregation improve recovery of recorded components and reduce ratio prediction error.

Forty-Third International Conference on Machine Learning

OSM+: Billion-Level OpenStreetMap Dataset for City-wide Experiments [paper][code]

Guanjie Zheng, Ziyang Su, Yiheng Wang, Yuhang Luo, Hongwei Zhang, Xuanhe Zhou, Linghe Kong*, Fan Wu, Wen Ling*

ICML2026 (CCF-A)·Spotlight·First student author

OSM+ provides a worldwide, billion-scale road-network graph dataset with a distributed preprocessing pipeline and SQL-style spatial queries for accessing topology, geometry, and attributes. It also supplies a 31-city traffic-prediction benchmark and six city-scale traffic-policy-control datasets to support city-wide graph learning and scalability research.

The 30th International Conference on Database Systems for Advanced Applications

Chordal Graph Sampling-Based Mini-batch Training Algorithm for Large Graphs [paper][code]

Ziyang Su, Wentao He, Jiayuan Liu

DASFAA2025 (CCF-B)·First author

CGST combines balanced chordal graph partitioning with batch-wise random aggregation for mini-batch GNN training, restoring inter-cluster connections to reduce structural information loss from partitioning. Experiments on three large-scale graph datasets evaluate node classification and training scalability.

The 29th International Conference on Database Systems for Advanced Applications

POI-based Traffic Generation via Supervised Contrastive Learning on Reconstructed Graph [paper][code]

Ziyang Su, Zhanyu Liu, Jianrong Ding, Guanjie Zheng*

DASFAA2024 (CCF-B)·First author

TG-SCR reconstructs a k-nearest-neighbor graph from POI information and aggregates features to mitigate road-network structural bias when generating traffic for areas without historical traffic inputs. Supervised contrastive learning based on traffic-flow similarity shapes the POI representations, with evaluation on four real-world datasets.

Internships

  1. Tencent WXG R&D Department

    2025.10 to Present

    Smart Business Lab | Project Intern

    Research on Credit Users' Lifetime Value Prediction

    Developed cumulative spending prediction for BiBiSheng's e-commerce and food-delivery businesses using approximately 4.85 million users and 41.84 million transactions, covering 8 targets across 7-, 14-, 30-, and 45-day horizons. Designed a graph-enhanced dual-business contrastive sequence model combining a within-batch user-item bipartite graph, business-specific Transformers, cross-view contrastive learning, and a zero-inflated mixture log-normal (ZIMLN) head to address sparse behavior, heavy-tailed spending, and missing item information. In comparative evaluations against MDAN, reduced MAE by 22.50% and 16.78% for the 14- and 30-day e-commerce targets, and by 4.86% and 5.92% for the 30- and 45-day food-delivery targets, respectively; used module ablations and later-window evaluations to assess component contributions and temporal generalization.

    Exploring SID Features for Product Recommendation

    Integrated SID as a feature into a product recommendation model and compared purchase-task performance with and without SID. AUC increased from 0.7754 to 0.8258 (6.49% relative improvement), while GAUC improved by 0.78% relative; LogLoss decreased from 0.04819 to 0.04421 (8.25% relative reduction), demonstrating improved purchase-probability estimation.

  2. China Yangtze Power Group Co., Ltd.

    2025.05 to 2025.07

    Architecture and Solution Design for Intelligent Hydropower Operations and Maintenance | Intern

    Translated requirements for hydropower equipment inspection and river-basin scheduling into technical solutions for data governance, model management, and intelligent interaction. Analyzed early-warning model failures associated with stuck sensor readings, interruptions, and changing operating conditions, and detailed module responsibilities for multi-source time-series cleaning and annotation, general-purpose models, MLOps, and RAG-assisted model recommendation. Designed an expert-agent collaboration workflow spanning natural-language requirement interpretation, model selection, and evaluation feedback, specifying how datasets, model libraries, and operations platforms connect. Collaboratively developed a five-layer technical architecture, a key project list, and technical roadmaps to support technology selection and feasibility assessment for intelligent inspection and maintenance.

  3. Hangzhou Yunqi Institute of Engineering

    2022.06 to 2022.09

    Construction and Standardization of Large-Scale Urban Road-Network Graph Datasets | Intern

    Constructed standardized graph datasets for city-scale graph learning and traffic modeling by splitting raw OpenStreetMap road polylines into segments with explicit endpoints, organizing node identifiers, road connectivity, and POI information, and building node and road-segment tables. Used SQL spatial-range queries in MaxCompute (ODPS) to extract city road-network subgraphs and organize node-to-segment associations, covering data parsing, graph structuring, and city subgraph extraction to provide reusable datasets for traffic prediction, traffic control, and city-boundary detection.
